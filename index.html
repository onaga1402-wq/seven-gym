Our story

2018 – The garage

Seven friends, one rented garage and a single squat rack. We trained at 5 a.m. and promised to open a real gym.

2021 – The first hall

We opened our doors with 400 members in the first month, all from word of mouth.

Today – A legacy in progress

A full facility, a coaching team and a community that pushes each other to finish what they start.
